# Changelog

## 0.0.1

* Initial release of `datepickr`.
* Support for Date, Date Range, Month & Year, and Year pickers.
* Custom compact dialog and standard fullscreen modal options for Date Range Picker.
* Grid and wheel slider styles for Month-Year and Year pickers.
* Responsive dialog layouts for portrait and landscape modes across mobile and tablet.
* Theme-agnostic styling with Material 3 and Material 2 compatibility.
* Boundary constraints and `onlyCompletedYears` filtering.
* Full internationalization and localization support.
